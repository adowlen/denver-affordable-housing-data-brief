## LinkedIn draft (held for Friday synthesis)

29,474 income-restricted homes. 15 neighborhoods with none.

Denver's HOST inventory lists 497 income-restricted projects holding 29,474 units. Five Points holds 2,757 of them. The inventory records no project in 15 of the city's 78 neighborhoods, including Washington Park, Hilltop, and North Park Hill.

91.3% of the units are rentals. 2,628 are supportive units. And one neighborhood breaks the rate table: Sun Valley lists 1,196 restricted units against an ACS base of 503 housing units, so we print its counts and not its rate.

Full brief, methods, and data: https://github.com/adowlen/denver-affordable-housing-data-brief
